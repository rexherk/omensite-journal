How the hosted journal stores your data

At journal.omensite.com, the application uses a managed PostgreSQL connection. Trading records, written reviews, account settings, sign-in sessions, and uploaded chart images are saved in that database. Temporary CSV import previews are stored on the server until confirmation or cleanup.

Discord sign-in and access

Discord provides your account ID, username, display name, avatar information, and email when supplied. Omensite uses that identity to create or find your journal. Session cookies keep you signed in, and a separate demo cookie opens the shared sample workspace.

Journal routes check which user owns the requested records. Hosting operators and authorized database administrators manage the underlying service and its storage.

Export your records

Settings provides a CSV trade-history export, a JSON backup, and a print view. JSON includes trade fills, notes, tags, checklists, account settings, and saved chart images. CSV contains trade fields and notes. Export downloads require a signed-in or demo session and use private, no-store cache headers. Keep your downloaded copies somewhere you trust.

Delete individual records

You can delete a trade from its detail page or remove a chart attachment. Deleting a trade removes its fills, tags, checklist answers, linked screenshots, and associated image data from the application’s active storage. An account containing trades must have those trades removed before the account can be deleted.

The application does not currently offer a whole-user account deletion control. Hosting backups and operational logs have separate retention settings. This application does not specify a verified retention period for those copies.

Demo and help

The demo uses shared simulated records and is read-only. Its results demonstrate the journal workflow. The help button opens an on-page guide.

Tradays economic calendar

The News page automatically loads a calendar from Tradays, a service operated by MetaQuotes. Tradays may collect your IP address and browser details and use cookies. See the Tradays privacy policy.

Support and account-closure requests

Discord support contact · Username reelrex.

Contact reelrex on Discord for help or to request closure of your Omensite Journal account. Ask support to confirm the handling and completion of your request. Hosting backup and log retention details still require administrator confirmation.

Running a local copy

A local installation uses SQLite in its configured data folder when no PostgreSQL connection is provided. Older local chart-image files remain supported. Storage for a local copy is controlled by the person running that server.
